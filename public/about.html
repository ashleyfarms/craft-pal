About Craft Pal

Craft Pal helps people who sell handmade goods work out what each item really costs to make and what it earns. Enter your materials once, build a recipe for each product, and log sales as they happen.

What you can do

  • Keep a list of materials with what you paid and how much you get per pack.
  • Build recipes so a wreath or a shirt adds up its own cost.
  • Log sales from markets, Etsy or Facebook and see profit after materials.
  • See which products earn the most for your time.

It works offline, so you can log a sale at a craft fair with no signal.

Free, supported by ads

Craft Pal is free to use. There is nothing to buy and no subscription. We keep it free by showing a small number of Google ads. Your entries are stored on your own device unless a feature clearly says otherwise.
